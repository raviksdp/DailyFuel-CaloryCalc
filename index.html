GOOD QUESTIONS

A little more clarity.

Where should I start?

Explore the coaching approach above, then try the free calorie calculator to get an estimated baseline. Think about your current routine and one change you’d like to work on.

Do I need to count every calorie?

The calculator is an optional tool for understanding your estimated energy needs. Coaching also makes room for your preferences, daily routine, and the habits you want to build.

Does coaching guarantee a specific result?

No. Everyone’s starting point, circumstances, and progress are different. Coaching focuses on practical steps and ongoing reflection rather than a promised number or deadline.
