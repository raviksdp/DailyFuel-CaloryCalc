A little science behind the numbers

We use the Mifflin–St Jeor equation to estimate resting calorie needs, then account for your activity level.

◎

A starting point, not a strict rule

These are estimates for adults. Your needs are unique—adjust with your progress, energy levels, and professional guidance.

♡

More than a number

Balanced meals, good sleep, and movement you enjoy all play a part. Give yourself room to find what works.

How are my results calculated?

Resting energy = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age + a sex-based constant (+5 for male, −161 for female). Maintenance uses your activity multiplier: 1.2, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725, or 1.9. Weight-loss and weight-gain estimates apply a 15% deficit and a 10% surplus. Results are rounded to the nearest calorie and are not a prediction of weight change. This adult calculator is not intended for pregnancy or breastfeeding.
