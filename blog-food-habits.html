FOOD & ROUTINE

Make everyday meals a little easier

A practical way to plan a few familiar meals, simplify shopping, and leave room for the food you enjoy.

r.By Ravi · 3 min read

When you’re working toward a weight-loss goal, it’s easy to turn every meal into a decision that needs to be perfect. Instead, try asking a smaller question: what would make eating at home a little easier this week?

Start with what you already enjoy

Write down three meals you like and know how to make. They could be dal and rice, eggs on toast, or a favourite stir-fry. You’re looking for familiar options you can return to, rather than a completely new way of eating.

For each meal, think about the ingredients you usually have and what you might like to add. A meal doesn’t have to be elaborate to deserve a place in your routine.

Plan for the busy day

A plan that only works when you have an hour to cook leaves the busiest days unanswered. Choose one backup meal that uses a few pantry, frozen, or ready-to-use ingredients. Keep those ingredients on your shopping list.

For example, your backup might be a chickpea wrap with chopped vegetables and yogurt, or leftover rice with tofu and frozen vegetables. Choose something you actually want to eat.

Prepare one useful thing

Meal preparation doesn’t need to mean identical containers for the whole week. Pick one task that removes friction: wash some vegetables, make a shopping list, or set aside the ingredients for tomorrow’s breakfast. Decide which task would help you most before doing more.

Keep room for flexibility

Some days you’ll eat out. Some meals will happen later than planned. That doesn’t make the rest of your routine irrelevant. Return to a familiar meal when it suits your day, rather than trying to compensate for an unplanned choice.

Try this this week

Choose three familiar meals and one backup. Write a short shopping list, then notice which option made your week easier.
